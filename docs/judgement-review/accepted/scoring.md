# Who was the better scorer: Kobe in 2005-06 or Curry in 2015-16?

# Scoring Claim Review

## Answer

**My judgement: I lean toward Stephen Curry.**

**Criterion:** Evaluating scoring effectiveness through volume production relative to efficiency standards.

**Decisive reason:** Stephen Curry achieved superior scoring efficiency, recording a substantially higher raw true shooting percentage and contemporaneous relative true shooting advantage compared to Kobe Bryant. [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/playercareerstats?PlayerID=977&PerMode=Totals&LeagueID=00) [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/playercareerstats?PlayerID=201939&PerMode=Totals&LeagueID=00) [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/leaguegamelog?Counter=0&Direction=ASC&LeagueID=00&PlayerOrTeam=T&Season=2005-06&SeasonType=Regular+Season&Sorter=DATE&DateFrom=&DateTo=) [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/leaguegamelog?Counter=0&Direction=ASC&LeagueID=00&PlayerOrTeam=T&Season=2015-16&SeasonType=Regular+Season&Sorter=DATE&DateFrom=&DateTo=)

**Strongest counterargument:** Kobe Bryant delivered higher accumulated points and a larger points per game output, maintaining greater absolute scoring volume across the regular season. [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/playercareerstats?PlayerID=977&PerMode=Totals&LeagueID=00) [Evidence](https://stats.nba.com/stats/playercareerstats?PlayerID=201939&PerMode=Totals&LeagueID=00)

**Why this judgement:** The comparison balances Stephen Curry's clear efficiency and relative scoring advantage against Kobe Bryant's higher total points and points per game output.

**What could change it:** Additional efficiency metrics or usage context could alter the balance, but current shooting efficiency metrics favor Stephen Curry.
Claim assessment: Conditional: the broad claim is not uniformly supported across the selected metrics.
**Regular Season: Kobe Bryant leads total points, points per game; Stephen Curry leads true shooting, league-relative efficiency.**

**Scope:** 2005-06–2005-06 versus 2015-16–2015-16; Regular Season.

> Assess whether Kobe Bryant scored more strongly in 2005-06 than Stephen Curry in 2015-16.

- Subjects: **Kobe Bryant** and **Stephen Curry**.
- Scope: 2005-06–2005-06 versus 2015-16–2015-16; Regular Season.
