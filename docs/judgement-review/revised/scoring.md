# Who was the better scorer: Kobe in 2005-06 or Curry in 2015-16?

# Scoring Claim Review

## Answer

**Split scoring verdict: the selected rate-and-efficiency measures do not support one winner.**
The advantages below depend on the metric or phase. Choosing an overall winner would require an additional priority, rather than a conclusion established by these data.
Claim assessment: Conditional: the broad claim is not uniformly supported across the selected metrics.
**Regular Season: Kobe Bryant leads total points, points per game; Stephen Curry leads true shooting, league-relative efficiency.**
**Playoffs: Kobe Bryant leads points per game; Stephen Curry leads total points, true shooting, league-relative efficiency.**

**Scope:** 2005-06–2005-06 versus 2015-16–2015-16; Regular Season, Playoffs.

## Scope and methods

> Assess whether Kobe Bryant in 2005-06 scored more strongly than Stephen Curry in 2015-16

- Subjects: **Kobe Bryant** and **Stephen Curry**.
- Scope: 2005-06–2005-06 versus 2015-16–2015-16; Regular Season, Playoffs.
- Statistical lens: overall scoring.
- Completed-season cutoff: **2025-26**; ongoing seasons are excluded.
- Metrics use summed source inputs. Missing observations remain missing.
- Relative efficiency uses same-season, same-phase league TS; aggregation weights each season by FGA + 0.44 × FTA.
