# Who helped their teammates more in 2015-16, Curry or LeBron?

# On-Court Impact Review

## Answer

**My judgement: I lean toward Stephen Curry.**

**Criterion:** Evaluating playmaking and team on-minus-off impact in the 2015-16 regular season.

**Decisive reason:** Using the regular season as the primary phase, Stephen Curry recorded higher assists per 36 minutes and a stronger on-minus-off net rating compared to LeBron James. Stephen Curry helped his team achieve a larger positive scoring margin when on the court versus the bench. **Why this judgement:** Stephen Curry provided higher raw assist volume per 36 minutes in the regular season, while LeBron James maintained a slightly better assists per turnover ratio during that same phase.

**What could change it:** Shifting priority entirely to the playoff phase or emphasizing ball security over assist volume would reverse the preference to LeBron James.

## Dimension findings

- **Playmaking and ball security:** The indicators disagree; one player does not dominate all selected measures.
- **Team on/off context:** Regular Season: Stephen Curry has the higher on-minus-off net rating; Playoffs: LeBron James has the higher on-minus-off net rating. This describes team association, not an isolated player effect.

This position compares observed contributions, not innate ability. Playmaking uses assists per minute and assists/turnovers, not a complete model of creation. Team on/off is association rather than causation.

**Scope:** 2015-16–2015-16 versus 2015-16–2015-16; Regular Season, Playoffs.

## Coverage and limits

- Historical box-score evidence is not equivalent to modern tracking or lineup data. Missing historical coverage does not count against a player.
- Steals, blocks and rebounds describe recorded activity; matchup difficulty, positioning, deterrence and defensive schemes are not measured here.
- On/off comparisons are confounded by lineup, opponent and substitution choices. No causal team/winning impact is established.
- Small or unequal appearance samples and stale API responses remain limitations. No synthetic overall impact score is used.
